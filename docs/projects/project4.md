# Sales & Marketing Reporting Modernisation

> Replaced manual report runs with dynamic self-serve dashboards and built source-of-truth datamarts for Sales and Marketing.

## The Problem

Sales and Marketing teams at BMW Financial Services relied on manually generated reports with inconsistent definitions across teams. There was no single source of truth for key business metrics. Stakeholders had to request reports from the analytics team for every question, creating bottlenecks and delays. The reporting infrastructure needed modernising to support self-serve analytics.

## What I Did

- **Migrated priority reports** from legacy formats to dynamic, interactive Tableau dashboards that stakeholders could explore independently.
- **Built and extended source-of-truth datamarts** (including Contracts and VFI datamarts) to standardise metric definitions across the organisation.
- **Delivered self-serve dashboards** that gave Sales and Marketing teams direct access to the data they needed, without waiting on the analytics team.
- **Established clear data definitions** to eliminate ambiguity around how key business metrics were calculated and reported.

## Tech Stack

`Tableau` · `SQL` · `Data Marts` · `Data Warehousing` · `Data Governance`

## Results

- **Eliminated reliance on manual report runs** for routine Sales and Marketing analytics.
- Enabled stakeholder self-serve, reducing the analytics team's ad-hoc request load.
- Created a trusted, governed data foundation that other reporting initiatives could build on.
