# Enterprise AI Enablement & Training Assistants

> Drove sustained AI adoption across the organisation through workshops, champion networks, and department-specific training chat assistants.

## The Problem

BMW Financial Services rolled out a new internal enterprise AI platform for building conversational assistants. The technology was ready, but adoption was the real challenge. Without a structured enablement strategy, the platform risked becoming another deployed tool that nobody used. Staff needed to understand not just how to use it, but why it mattered for their day-to-day work.

## What I Did

- **Designed and facilitated organisation-wide workshops** covering AI literacy, prompt engineering, and context engineering principles, tailored for non-technical audiences.
- **Established an AI champion network** of super users trained in advanced platform features, who became the go-to resource within their teams.
- **Led champions to build Training Chat Assistants** by ingesting Standard Operating Procedures and department documentation, creating accurate, contextualised onboarding tools for each team.
- **Ran continuous follow-up check-ins** to resolve blockers and sustain adoption beyond the initial rollout.

## Tech Stack

`Enterprise GenAI Platform` · `Prompt Engineering` · `Context Engineering` · `Change Management`

## Results

- **Reduced staff onboarding time** by delivering complete, contextualised answers to new joiners through department-specific chat assistants.
- **Decreased repetitive queries** directed at senior team members, freeing up experienced staff for higher-value work.
- **Sustained adoption** through champion network and ongoing support, not a one-off training event.
