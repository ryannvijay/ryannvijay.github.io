# Real-Time CIC Dashboard Enhancement

> Transformed customer service reporting from a monthly cycle to daily, contributing to a 30% uplift in Net Promoter Score.

## The Problem

Customer Service reporting at BMW Financial Services was highly manual and locked to a slow monthly cycle. By the time insights reached decision-makers, the data was weeks old. The team could not make agile, data-driven decisions about customer experience because they were always looking in the rearview mirror.

## What I Did

- **Led the initiative to shift reporting cadence from monthly to daily**, giving the Customer Services team near real-time visibility into performance.
- **Partnered with the RPA team** to design automations for daily data extraction, replacing manual pull processes.
- **Engineered back-end ETL jobs** to aggregate the new daily data pipeline into a structure that supported granular analysis.
- **Enhanced front-end Tableau dashboards** to display highly detailed metrics across multiple KPIs, enabling self-serve analysis at a level that was not previously possible.

## Tech Stack

`Tableau` · `RPA` · `ETL Pipelines` · `SQL` · `Data Warehousing`

## Results

- **30% uplift in Net Promoter Score (NPS)** as the Customer Services team could steer activities proactively rather than react to month-old data.
- Empowered the team to identify and act on trends daily, not quarterly.
- Replaced a fragile, manual reporting process with a reliable automated pipeline.
